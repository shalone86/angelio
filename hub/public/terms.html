angelio.org · plain-language terms

Angelio offers free learning games for children at angelio.org. Some games offer optional “speech checks”, which a parent or guardian can extend with a paid family code.

Who can buy

Family codes must be bought by an adult (18+), for use by their family.

What you get

A family code gives up to 300 speech checks per calendar month, for as long as your subscription is active. Unused checks don't carry over. We may change the games and features, but if we ever reduce what a paid code includes, we'll tell you first and you can cancel for a refund of the unused time.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel anytime from the link in your receipt; your code keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.

Fair use

One code is for one family. Please don't share it publicly or resell it. We may turn off a code that's being misused, and we'll refund unused time if we do.

Free features

The free games and free daily speech checks are offered as-is and may change.

Not a medical assessment

Speech checks are a learning aid, not a medical or speech-therapy assessment. For speech concerns, please talk with a qualified speech-language pathologist.

Refunds

If you're not happy, email us within 30 days of a payment and we'll refund it in full, no questions asked. After 30 days you can cancel anytime and you won't be charged again.
