🗣️ Family voices

Family and friends record the lines every game says most, and kids pick whose voice reads to them (on angelio.org and in each game's grown-ups page). Add a person, then send them their own link: they record on their phone, no key needed. A family-only voice (a child's, say) is heard only on devices with a family code. Ask each person first, and use first names ("Grandma Rose").
