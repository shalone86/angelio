How to type it

Paste one of your old sheets as it is (chords on the line above the words), or put chords inside the words: [G]The Eu[F]charist. Section names go on their own line: VERSE 1, CHORUS, INTRO. A section name again with nothing under it means "sing it again". A line of only chords is played (an intro). Loop: C G F Dm/C sets a section's vamp yourself.

For the melody: a hyphen splits a word into its sung parts, one note each (spe-cial, Eu-cha-rist), and _ holds a word for one more note. The hyphens don't show on the sheet.

Melody

Pick which section the tune is for (usually the first verse and the chorus), then load a MIDI file from Logic (just the melody track) or play it on your MIDI keyboard.
