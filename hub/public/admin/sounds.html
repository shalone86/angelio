Whose voice?

Each of you records the whole list. In the apps, grown-ups pick Mom's or Dad's voice (⚙️); a sound only one of you recorded uses that one, and the robot says the rest.

Say only the big sound (for sss, just “sssss”), not the example words under it: those only show which sound is meant, and the games add “like sun” themselves.
Tips: a quiet room, phone about a hand away. Press ⏺, say just the sound, short and clear (for t a quick "t" with only a whisper of air, not "tuh-uh"), then it stops by itself. Silence before and after is trimmed for you, and the volume is evened out. Not happy? Record it again.

Lines are the sentences the games say: the "How it works" cards, "Great job!", "Try again!" and the finish screen. Read each one like you're talking to your child, at an easy pace. Where a word is in color, say that sound (just the sound, like in the Sounds list). Press ⏺ Record, read it, then ⏹ Stop. A little each day adds up: every line you record plays in your voice right away, and the rest stay with the robot.
