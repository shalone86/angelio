🎙️ Record your voice

Thank you for lending your voice! Kids playing the Angelio learning games can pick you to read to them. Each line is something the games say a lot, like praise when an answer is right, or a gentle "try again".

How: find a quiet spot, tap ⏺ Record, say the line warmly (like you're talking to a 5-year-old you love), then tap ⏹ Stop. Listen with ▶ and record again if you like. The silence at the start and end is cut for you. You can stop any time and come back to this same link later.
