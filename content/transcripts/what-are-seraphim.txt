Today we will talk about the Seraphim and their importance in Christian theology. A man named Isaiahs went to a special place called the Temple. He had a very important vision in Isaiah chapter 6 verse 2. He saw things that were not visible to everyone else. He saw angels called Seraphim who served God and they had six wings. They called out to each other praising God and saying, Holy, Holy, Holy, Lord God of hosts. All the earth is full of his glory. One of the angels even touched Isaiahs lips with a lump of hot coal, making him feel unique and inspired. The Seraphim are like a big choir that sings so loud it shakes the whole place they are in. Even though Isaiah said they looked slightly like humans, they are spiritual beings without bodies. Some people think they are not real but Catholics believe, based on the Bible and writings from the saints, that they are real angels. There are many types of angels in heaven. Two of them are called Seraphim and Cherubim. The Cherubim help show God's glory on earth while Seraphim help God in the heavenly court. Seraphim are known for carrying celestial fire to purify things. Some people think their name means fiery and flying serpent, but we can't know for sure. Seraphim are very important in Christian beliefs and are part of the highest rank in the heavenly hierarchy. In the Bible and in some hymns they are shown singing Holy, Holy, Holy. In conclusion we can understand more about the Seraphim by reading the book Isaiahs. These angels are the top servants of God and have amazing features like six wings and the power to purify with fire. They are even higher up than the Cherubim. The Seraphim always sing about how great and holy God is, showing their devotion to serving Him. And that was Seraphim in a nutshell. Thanks for learning with us.
