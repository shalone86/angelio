A nativity scene, which is sometimes called a creche or a manger, is a depiction of the birth of Jesus Christ as described in the Bible. It typically includes figures of Mary, Joseph, and the baby Jesus, the shepherds, the three wise men, and various animals. The custom of the nativity scene or displaying figures depicting the birth of Jesus Christ owes its origin to St. Francis of Assisi, who made the Christmas creche or manger, or Christmas Eve in the year of 1223.
