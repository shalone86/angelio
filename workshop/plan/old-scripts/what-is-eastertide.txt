# What is Eastertide?
# The old video's words (speech-to-text, Nov 2026; small mistakes possible). Watch & Sing id: what-is-eastertide, made 2023-06-12.

Pascal Tide, also known as Easter Tide or the Easter season, is a special time in the Church when we
celebrate the resurrection of Jesus Christ. It lasts from Easter Sunday until Pentecost. This
celebration is marked by joyful liturgical customs, white vestments, reciting the Tadeum and Gloria,
and saying, the Alleluia. During this time, the Church requires us to receive Holy Communion, which
is an important part of our faith. Welcome to SD Case & Course's Today's Topic is Pascal Tide.
Pascal Tide lasts for different periods in different parts of the world, but it is always a time to
celebrate our faith and the joy it brings. The tradition of the Roman Church was to celebrate Pascal
Tide from Easter Sunday until Pentecost. During this Easter season, the Church has a rule that
everyone who can should take communion. Chapter 1389 of the Catholic Catechism states, quote, The
Church obliges the faithful to receive the Eucharist at least once a year, if possible, during the
Easter season. But the Church strongly encourages the faithful to receive the Holy Eucharist on
Sundays and feast days, or more often still, even daily. Unquote. That means that, if you've had
your first communion, you have to take communion during the Easter season, and according to the
Catechism, we should be taking communion as often as possible, even daily. But don't forget that you
should regularly go to confession as well. We can learn from Pascal Tide that it is important to
actively participate in our faith and share in its joy with others. And that was Pascal Tide in a
nutshell. Thanks for learning with us. Until next time, may God bless you forever and ever.
