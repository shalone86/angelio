Velodomir Sketoslavik, also known as Velodomir the Great, was the Prince of Novgorod from the year
970 and Grand Prince of Kyiv from the year 978 until his death in the year 1015. He became a
Christian in the year 987 and Christianized his country by building churches and giving to the poor.
He is considered a canonized saint by the Catholic Church, Eastern Orthodox Church, and Byzantine
Lutherans. Today we are learning about Velodomir the Great, a powerful ruler who Christianized
Kyivin Rus and made it a center for politics and culture in Europe. Let's begin with the entire
story of Velodomir the Great. A long time ago, Rurik was the ruler of Novgorod, a northwestern
region of Russia near the Volga River. Rurik was one of the first rulers of Novgorod and he put his
family members in charge after he died. One of those family members was Svyatoslav the First,
Velodomir's father. Velodomir's father ruled Novgorod for many years until he died in the year 972.
Five years later, Velodomir's brother Yarlapolk murdered their brother Oleg to become the King of
Rus. Then Velodomir ran away because he was worried that his brother might kill him as well. But
Velodomir did not just run and hide. No, he gathered an army of Vikings. Vikings were warriors who
sailed the sea looking for adventure and conquering new lands. So Vikings would make a perfect group
of warriors to challenge his brother for the throne. Velodomir returned to Rus and defeated his
brother in the year 978, just one year after he escaped. A few years after becoming king,
missionaries from many different religions came to Rus trying to convert Velodomir. He wasn't
impressed by any of them except Christianity. But Velodomir still didn't convert. It was only after
he wanted to marry the Christian Imperial Princess, Anna, that he finally decided to convert. After
he was baptized, he took the name Basil. After his wedding to Anna, Velodomir returned home and
built many Christian churches. He also gave out food and drinks to the less fortunate, established
schools, and protected poor people when he could. History spread in Kivion Rus because of his
example and dedication. So who was Velodomir the Great? He was the Prince of Novgorod from the year
970. He escaped his brother's war and returned with a Viking army to defeat him. He became Grand
Prince of Kiv from the year 978 until his death in 1015. In the year 987, he was baptized, became
Christian, and married the Byzantine Imperial Princess Anna. He Christianized his country by
building churches and giving to the poor. He is considered a canonized saint by the Catholic Church,
Eastern Orthodox Church, and Byzantine Lutherans. And that was Velodomir the Great in a nutshell.
