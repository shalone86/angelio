# What is a Jesse Tree?
# The old video's words (speech-to-text, Nov 2026; small mistakes possible). Watch & Sing id: what-is-a-jesse-tree, made 2023-11-22.

The tradition of decorating Christmas trees has a rich history that traces back to pre-Christian
times. Over the years, this practice has evolved and been embraced by the early church. To add a
deeper connection to Christianity, the concept of Jesse trees emerged. These special Christmas trees
are adorned with ornaments that symbolize the remarkable events surrounding the birth of Jesus and
the prophecies mentioned in the Old Testament. Nowadays, many parishes and families eagerly engage
in the creation of Jesse trees during Advent. It serves as a meaningful way to reflect on these
significant events while joyfully preparing for the celebration of Christmas. There isn't an
official set of ornaments or events for the Jesse tree, and you will find many different
combinations online. Also, Advent can vary from 22 to 28 days, so you should find what works best
for you and your family. That's the Jesse tree tradition in a nutshell. This has been S.D. Case and
