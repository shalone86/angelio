# Conditions of Prayer
# The old video's words (speech-to-text, Nov 2026; small mistakes possible). Watch & Sing id: conditions-of-prayer, made 2024-04-16.

Can we pray in any way? Can we be distracted or pray without really meaning it? What do we need to
do to make sure our prayers will be effective? Although Christ's promises about prayers are very
strong, there are some things to keep in mind to make sure that our prayers are effective. We need
to pray for things that are good for us and pleasing to God, whether that be in a spiritual or
practical sense. Welcome to SDKs and Courses. Today's topic is the conditions of prayer. Let's
begin. When someone prays, they should always be willing to accept whatever spiritual blessings God
may give them, and they should only desire earthly blessings if they can help them serve God better.
To pray effectively, we need to have faith and trust in God. This means not only believing that God
can answer our prayers, but also having confidence that God will keep His promise to hear our
specific prayer. We must have faith that if we ask God for something good for us, God will grant it,
or if it's not good for us, He will give us something else that is even better for us in His wisdom.
For prayer to work, it's important to be humble. Demanding things from God is not considered prayer.
The story of the Pharisee and the publican in the Bible clearly shows this, and that's in Luke
chapter 18 verses 9-14. The story is about two people who go to the temple to pray. One of them is a
Pharisee who thinks he is very good and better than everyone else. The other is a tax collector who
knows he's done wrong things and asks God for help. Jesus says that the tax collector was the better
person because he was humble and asked for forgiveness. Therefore we should be humble when we pray.
Many other examples in scripture tell us how humility and prayer can be very powerful, such as Psalm
1, quote, a contrite and humbled heart, oh God, thou wilt not despise, unquote. And Ecclesiastes 35,
quote, the prayer of him that humbleth himself shall pierce the clouds unquote. We should always try
to be humble and make sure that we are trying to stop sinning and to do good. These actions will
help us to pray better. However, our trust in God's kindness and the merits of Christ should always
be the main reason for our confidence in prayer. To pray effectively, we also need to be sincere. We
should try our best to achieve what we are praying for, and we shouldn't pray for things we don't
really want. When we pray, we should also avoid doing things that contradict our prayer. Lastly, we
should focus seriously on our prayers and try not to be distracted. Wanting God's will does not mean
that we should not care whether our prayers are answered or not. We should pray for things that we
need to follow God's will. If we use our full attention and focus on our prayers, God will give us
the grace to be with him in heaven. In Luke chapter 11, verses 5 through 8, Jesus tells a story
about a man who asked his friends for bread late at night. Even though the friend is hesitant at
first, he eventually gives the bread because the man keeps asking and asking. Through this story,
Jesus teaches us that it's important to keep praying and asking God for help, as he will listen to
us when we persistently seek him. So what conditions are needed for a good prayer? First we need to
be humble when we pray. Next we need to be sincere and ask for things we truly want and try to get
them. Next we have to pray with our full attention and focus. If you pray in this way, avoid sin and
do good, you will receive grace from God. And that's what prayer is all about, right? This has been
SD case and courses. If you have any questions about prayer, please contact your parish priest or a
trusted spiritual advisor. Until next time, may God bless you forever and ever.
