# What is Palm Sunday?
# The old video's words (speech-to-text, Nov 2026; small mistakes possible). Watch & Sing id: what-is-palm-sunday, made 2023-06-12.

Welcome kids! Today we're going to learn about a special day in the Catholic Church called Palm
Sunday. Palm Sunday is the beginning of Holy Week which is a very important time for Catholics. It's
a Sunday of the highest rank. During Palm Sunday there are many ceremonies that take place, like the
blessing of the palms, the procession, the Mass, and the singing of the Passion. The blessing of the
palms is when the priest blesses the branches of palm or olive on the altar. Then, the palms are
distributed to the people and carried in a procession around the church. During the procession,
people sing hymns and hold up their palms. They do this to remember the day when Jesus entered
Jerusalem and the people welcomed him by waving palm branches. After the procession, Mass is
celebrated and the principal feature of the Mass is the singing of the Passion. The Passion is the
story of Jesus' arrest, trial, and crucifixion. The palms that were blessed on Palm Sunday are also
used as a sacramental. They're often preserved in prominent places in the house, in the barns, and
in the fields. In some places where palms can't be found, branches of olive, boxe, elder, spruce, or
other trees are used instead. Palm branches are also used to decorate graves and they're even used
to make ashes for Ash Wednesday. So that's what Palm Sunday is all about. Remember, it's a day of
celebration and it's a time to remember Jesus' triumphant entry into Jerusalem. And who knows, maybe
one day you'll get to hold a palm branch during a Palm Sunday procession. Thanks for learning with
us. Until next time, may God bless you forever and ever.
