# What are Evil Angels?
# The old video's words (speech-to-text, Nov 2026; small mistakes possible). Watch & Sing id: what-are-evil-angels, made 2023-06-22.

Angels are usually thought of as good guys who help us and make us feel safe, but in Catholicism
there's another kind of angel that's not so nice. The Evil Angel. These angels were once good but
they turned against God and now work for the bad guy, Satan. They try to trick people and lead them
away from doing good things. In this lesson we'll learn about where the idea of Evil Angels came
from, what they do, and why it's important to know about them. Welcome to S.D. Kaysen courses. In
this lesson we'll explore the origins Evil Angels and what they do. In the Bible there are two
spiritual kingdoms, one for good and one for bad. The bad kingdom is led by the devil, who used to
be an angel but turned against God. In the Gospel of John, Jesus called him the Prince of this
world, because he tries to make people do bad things. In pictures the devil is often shown as a
dragon because of a story from the book of the apocalypse he's called the angel of the bottomless
pit, the dragon, and the old serpent, and he fought against Archangel Michael. The devil tries to
trick people into doing bad things but we can choose to do good instead. The first idea about a bad
angel comes from the book of Genesis, which tells us the story of Adam and Eve and how they
disobeyed God. That story also explains that there are bad angels in the world that try to hurt
people. It's important to know that there are good angels and bad angels in the world, and we always
have help from Jesus Christ and our guardian angel to do good things. There are also stories in the
Bible about how some people were affected by evil spirits. In one story, a king named Saul was said
to be possessed by an evil spirit, but we're not sure if that was really true or just a way of
saying he was acting badly. In another story, a spirit appeared and offered to make some people tell
lies and God allowed it to happen. In the book of Job, it says that even if a thousand bad angels
tried to hurt someone, they wouldn't be able to. Another part of the book says that if someone gets
rich by doing something bad, an angel will make them throw up all their riches and leave their
house. In the book of Job, we learn that bad angels can only do bad things to us if God lets them
so. If we follow Christ, we should not fear Satan or his bad angels, and we also learn that Satan
fell from heaven, and the main sources for this are Isaiah chapter 14 and Ezekiel chapter 28. Jesus
even talks about it in Luke chapter 10. Jesus says Satan fell from the sky like lightning. Other
parts of the Bible also talk about angels who help God keep things fair. It's important to remember
that even when bad things happen, it is not always the fault of evil angels, and that's angels in a
nutshell. In short, the devil convinced some other angels to join him in his bad ways, and they go
around doing bad things. But don't worry, God is in charge, and evil angels can only hurt us if God
allows it. If we stay close to Jesus Christ, we don't have to be scared of evil angels at all.
Thanks for taking this lesson, and may God bless you forever and ever.
