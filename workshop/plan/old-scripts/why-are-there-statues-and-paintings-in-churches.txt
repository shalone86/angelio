Why are there so many statues and paintings in some Catholic churches? This is the Catholic Q&A and
today's question is, why are there so many statues and paintings in some Catholic churches? Well,
because according to the general instruction of the Roman Missal, or Girm for short, sacred
buildings for divine worship should be truly worthy and beautiful and be signs and symbols of
heavenly realities. In short, sacred art is beautiful and it requires that churches should have
beautiful art to remind us of the beauty of heaven. You know, all this modern art seems pretty
subjective to me. I can't tell what's good or bad. I completely understand and that's why the church
has a few standards as to what is appropriate art. First, Girm, chapter 318 states that religious
art should include Christ, the Virgin Mary, or one of the saints. Secondly, Girm, chapter 289 states
that religious art should nourish faith and devotion when people see it. Basically, any images that
remind us of Christ and how we transform sinners into saints can be considered good art. Bad art is
anything that is offensive, disgusting, immoral, or depraved. Also, bad art is anything that is
mediocre and lacks artistic worth. Okay, so what if someone says they draw a picture of Jesus? And
it looks like they randomly splash paint on a canvas. That would not be considered good or
appropriate art. In fact, sacrosanctum conchillium chapter 124 states that it is the bishop's duty
to remove bad art from his diocese. So yeah, the church is serious about good art. Alright, I
understand the difference between good and bad art, but is art really that important? Yes, when we
create sacred art, we are imitating the creative powers of God. We are making something beautiful
that can lift others' minds to God. That is why the church has always supported artists such as
Michelangelo, Da Vinci, and Caravaggio. And the church still patronizes many artists to this very
day. Furthermore, when literacy wasn't widespread, sacred art was a great way for people to learn
and understand the faith, even though they couldn't read. Also, we are visual creatures. This
stimulates our minds much more than words on paper. So yeah, art is really important to Catholicism.
So you're saying that sacred art is important because it helps us think in Christ and heaven? You
got it! The Virgin Mary, the angels, and saints, all these things remind us of the power of Christ
to lift us to heaven if we only follow his commands. Sacred art expresses the faith and hope of the
church, and thus, it is extremely important for the body of Christ. And that is why we have so many
statues and paintings in Catholic churches. Do you have any quotes from the saints about sacred art?
Of course! Here's my favorite quote about Catholic art from St. John Damascene. The beauty of the
images moves me to contemplation, as a meadow delights the eyes and subtly infuses the soul with the
glory of God. And that's it! I hope you have a greater understanding and devotion for sacred art.
