# Who is Saint Michael the Archangel?

Did you know the name Michael means who is like God? Michael's name is mentioned four times in the
Bible. He is one of the most important angels and in a big battle in heaven, Michael and the good
angels fought against the devil and his followers. In the book of Daniel, Daniel asked God if the
Jews could return to Jerusalem, but an angel from the kingdom of Persia tried to stop him. Then
another angel named Michael came to help. Gabriel said that Michael is one of the most important
angels and a helper to Daniel and people like us. It's all written in Daniel's chapter 10 and 12.
Today we're going to talk about Saint Michael, who he is, and well, what he does. Welcome to SDKs
and Courses. Today's lesson is Saint Michael. In the letter of Saint Jude, Saint Michael is shown
arguing with the devil that we're fighting over the body of Moses. Saint Michael won the argument
and hid Moses' body so people wouldn't worship him like a hero. The devil tried to trick people into
finding Moses' body, but Saint Michael protected it. You see, Saint Michael's main job, other than
being a messenger of God, is to help and protect people and to fight against Satan. We further read
in the book of the Apocalypse that in this story, Saint Michael fights against a dragon with his
army of angels. The battle is said to happen at the end of time, just like the one that happened in
heaven at the beginning of time. Some believe Saint Michael guarded the entrance to Paradise to
protect the tree of life and help God's people receive the Ten Commandments. In short, Saint Michael
is a brave and mighty angel who has done many great things. Some people think Saint Michael is the
boss of all the angels while others believe he's one of the lower angels. Either way, he's a really
important angel. People call him Prince of the Heavenly Army and highest general. Saint Michael is
also known for caring for sick people. There are many stories about him, but one of the most famous
is how he made a healing spring appear in a city. People who bathed in that spring and asked Saint
Michael for help were said to have been cured of their sicknesses. Saint Michael was especially
loved in certain countries where he's known as an angelic healer. There are many special days
throughout the year when people celebrate Saint Michael, depending on where they live. People even
celebrate him in some places because he helped them win battles. In Egypt, people celebrate him on
the 12th of every month and in Rome, they celebrate him on September 29th and 30th. Overall, Saint
Michael is a very important and helpful angel and people still celebrate him today. And that was
Saint Michael the archangel in a nutshell. Thanks for learning with us. Until next time, may God
bless you forever and ever.
