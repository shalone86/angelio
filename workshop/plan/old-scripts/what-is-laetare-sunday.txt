# What is Laetare Sunday?
# The old video's words (speech-to-text, Nov 2026; small mistakes possible). Watch & Sing id: what-is-laetare-sunday, made 2023-06-12.

Do you know what Lent is? Lent is a special time of the year for Christians. It's a time when we
prepare ourselves for Easter, the day we celebrate Jesus rising from the dead. Lent starts about six
weeks before Easter, and it lasts for 40 days. This year, Lent started on February 22nd, and it will
end on April 9th. The dates for Lent change a little each year. During Lent, Christians do things
like pray more, give to charity, and fast. Fasting means not eating certain foods or not eating at
all for a certain amount of time. It's a way to show God that we're sorry for our sins and that we
want to be closer to Him. Now, there's a special Sunday during Lent called Leitare Sunday. It's the
fourth Sunday of Lent, and it's called Leitare Sunday because of the first words of the song we sing
during Mass. Leitare Jerusalem means rejoice, O Jerusalem. On this day we're allowed to feel a
little bit happier than usual because we're getting closer to Easter. We celebrate Leitare Sunday by
using flowers on the altar, wearing rose-colored vestments instead of purple, and playing the organ
during Mass. It's a way to remind us that even though Lent can be hard, there's still joy in our
hearts. So that's what Leitare Sunday is all about. It's a time to remember that even though we're
preparing ourselves for Easter, we can still find joy in our hearts. Whether you're Christian or
not, Lent is a great time to think about how you can be a better person and help others. So let's
all try to make the world a better place this Lenten season.
