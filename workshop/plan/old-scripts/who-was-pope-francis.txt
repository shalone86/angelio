# Who was Pope Francis?

Pope Francis was born Jorge Maria Bergoglio in Buenos Aires, Argentina. He grew up in a big family
with Italian roots. When he was young, he became a Jesuit priest. Eventually, he became a bishop.
And finally, in 2013, he became the pope for 12 years until he died at the age of 88. Welcome to
SDKs and Courses. Today's topic is, Who was Pope Francis? Let's begin in the name of the Father, and
of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen. Pope Francis was born Jorge Maria Bergoglio in Buenos
Aires, Argentina. He grew up in a big family with Italian roots. Before he became a priest, he
worked as a chemical technician. But he felt a special calling to help people. First becoming a
priest in the Jesuit order in 1958. Next he became the provincial superior, a fancy name for a
leader, of the Argentine Jesuits in 1973. Next he became Archbishop of Buenos Aires in 1998.
Finally, Pope John Paul II made him a cardinal in 2001. Sometime later, in 2013, something very
important happened to Jorge. He was chosen to be the next pope. He chose the name Francis because
Saint Francis of Assisi was known for his love of nature and helping the poor. Pope Francis was the
first pope from South America, and he was also the first pope who was a Jesuit. Pope Francis was
known for being very humble and nice to everyone, especially poor people. He cared a lot about
people who were struggling, and he wanted everyone to be treated fairly. He also talked a lot about
caring for the earth. It was his mission to promote peace, kindness, and understanding between all
people, even people who disagreed with him, and especially people who were not Christian. He wrote
four important letters called encyclicals. The first was named Light of the Faith, Lumenfide, and
that was written in 2013. It describes how we should have faith in Christ and how our faith can help
improve the whole world. His second letter was named Laudato Si in 2015. He explained how we should
care for the earth to keep the water and air clean and healthy for everyone. His third letter was
named Fratelli Tutti, and that was written in 2020. Fratelli Tutti means all brothers and sisters,
and it was his reminder that we should be kind and friendly to each other. Even if we look different
or live very far away or have different religions. His final letter was Dilexik Nos in 2024. This
letter addressed modern issues like people wanting to do everything without any help, many people
being too poor, and the harm that technology is causing. The pope believed that the love of Christ
would provide an answer to all of these problems and many more. After 12 years as pope, Pope Francis
died at the age of 88, April 21, 2025, which was one day after Easter, Easter Monday. And here is a
quote from his final Easter homily. Quote, Brothers and sisters, this is the greatest hope of our
life. We can live this poor, fragile, and wounded existence, clinging to Christ, because he has
conquered death. He conquers our darkness and he will conquer the shadows of the world to make us
live with him in joy forever. And that was Pope Francis in a nutshell. Thanks for learning with us.
