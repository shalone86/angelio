# What is Ash Wednesday?
# The old video's words (speech-to-text, Nov 2026; small mistakes possible). Watch & Sing id: what-is-ash-wednesday, made 2023-06-12.

Ash Wednesday is a special day that happens during a time of year called Lent. On this day, people
go to church and the priest puts ashes in the shape of a cross on their forehead. The ashes come
from burning the palms that were blessed on Palm Sunday the year before. This is a reminder that we
are all made from dust and one day we will return to dust. Ash Wednesday reminds us that we should
be sorry for the wrong things we have done and try to be better people during Lent. It is a time to
think about how we can be kinder, more loving and more helpful to others. So, Ash Wednesday is an
important day for many people around the world and it helps us remember to always try to be perfect
as our Heavenly Father is perfect and that is Ash Wednesday in a nutshell. Thanks for learning with
us. Until next time, may God bless you forever and ever.
