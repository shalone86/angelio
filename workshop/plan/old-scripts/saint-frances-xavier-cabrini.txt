# Saint Frances Xavier Cabrini
# The old video's words (speech-to-text, Nov 2026; small mistakes possible). Watch & Sing id: saint-frances-xavier-cabrini, made 2024-03-16.

Mother Cabrini, our first United States Saint, was canonized as St. Francis Cabrini in July 1946.
After an unselfish life devoted to the cause of Christ, she died in 1917 after founding 67
hospitals, homes, and institutions. St. Francis Cabrini was born in Italy. She lived and worked at
an orphanage. Someday I will help you manage this lovely home. Someday you will, I hope. Off there I
shall go to China as a missionary someday. And where is China? But she didn't go to China. First,
she became a school teacher. Reverend Mother, I wish to join the sisters. It is too hard for you,
Francis. Perhaps later. As time passed, Francis became a nun and acted as manager of the home. Your
Excellency, I want to be a missionary. Yes, my child. But there is important work for you. I want
you to start a new order of missionary sisters. Mother Cabrini started the missionary order of the
Sisters of the Sacred Heart. Nine years later, Pope Leo XIII sent for her. Your Holiness, I want to
bring Jesus to China. Not China, but the United States. Many there have great need of you. Thousands
of Italians were coming to America. They needed help in the strange country. This will be our new
home. She arrived in New York to find plans gone wrong. I am sorry. The place is not ready. Very
well. I will find my own building for a hospital. This will make a good clinic for poor children.
Others joined Mother Cabrini. Her work spread and was successful. Nothing can stop my work for God.
You'd better rest. Your health won't stand the strain of constant work. She made her way over the
mountains of South America. The path is blocked. Come, sister. We'll walk around. She made endless
journeys between countries, leading her missionary sisters everywhere. God will see us through.
She's a brave woman. This is her 24th crossing. Despite her frailty, she was fearless. Mind the
sister. Now. Businessman respected her judgment. We were planning on building in this area. No. I
believe we'll build here. You'll see it will be better for our needs. Mother Cabrini's health
failed, and in 1917, she died in Chicago. Plans for her beatification were begun under Pope Pius XI.
I am very glad to venerate Blessed Mother Cabrini in her glory, remembering her as we knew her, in
her meekness, notwithstanding which she was able to do a great work for God and the salvation of
souls. Later, Blessed Mother Cabrini was canonized and is numbered among the saints as Saint Francis
Xavier Cabrini.
