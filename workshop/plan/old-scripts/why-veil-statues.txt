# Why Veil Statues?
# The old video's words (speech-to-text, Nov 2026; small mistakes possible). Watch & Sing id: why-veil-statues, made 2024-03-18.

Why do we cover statues during the final weeks of Lent? Well, there are a few reasons. Firstly,
covering the statues, or veiling them, shows that it is a special time of preparing for Easter
Sunday, and it should raise our minds towards heaven. But there's a lot more to say. Welcome to
SDCase and Courses. Today's topic is... Vailing Statues During Lent. Let's begin! In the United
States, during the fifth Sunday of Lent, it is common to cover up crosses and images in churches.
Crosses are kept covered until the end of the Lord's Passion on Good Friday, while images are
covered up until the beginning of the Easter Vigil. This practice is mentioned in the Roman Missal.
During the last few weeks of Lent, the Catholic Church suggests covering statues and images to help
us prepare for Easter Sunday. This may seem strange, but it is meant to help us feel more excited
and eager for the special day. We usually look at these images to help us think about God and
heaven, but covering them up can help us appreciate them even more. We use veils in church to remind
us that we are in a special time. When we see everything covered, we know that something is
different. These two weeks before Easter are a time to get ready for the Tritium, which is Holy
Thursday, Good Friday, and Holy Saturday, and the veils remind us of that. Secondly, veils help us
to feel more excited about Easter Sunday. It's like a surprise gift that we can't wait to open. When
we attend Daily Mass and see the veils, we feel curious about what's underneath them. Lastly, the
veils focus our attention on the words being said at Mass. When we listen to the passion narrative,
our senses are allowed to focus on the striking words from the Gospel and truly enter into the
scene. The main idea is that things that are hidden or covered should eventually be revealed. This
is because it's not natural for them to stay hidden forever. The statues are unveiled before the
Easter Vigil and it is a good reminder of our life on Earth. We live in a world where things are not
always clear and we are away from our true home. But when we are saved by Jesus Christ through the
sacraments, the unclear things become clear and we can see the beauty in everything around us. This
is a special custom that we can see in our local church, but we can also try it at home with our
family. We can cover important religious pictures with a veil. This is a beautiful tradition and it
will make this time of year more meaningful for the whole family. And that is veiling images in a
nutshell. This has been SDKs and Courses. If you have any further questions or concerns about this
topic, please contact your local parish priest or a trusted spiritual advisor. Until next time, may
God bless you, forever and ever.
