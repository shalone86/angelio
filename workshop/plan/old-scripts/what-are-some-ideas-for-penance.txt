So, what's a good penance? This is the Catholic Q&A and today's question is, what is a good penance?
Well, in order to be a good penitent, you need to know what sins you've committed. So you have to
start with an examination of conscience. Go through the Ten Commandments and the Seven Deadly Sins.
Have you been involved in any of these sinful activities in any manner? If so, go to confession and
the priest should give you a proper penance based on your sins. Of course, if you want to show God
you're really sorry, you can do additional penance. Some common forms of penance are saying the Our
Father, saying three Hail Marys, doing the Stations of the Cross, fasting, that is eating only one
meal a day, saying the Rosary, and reading the Gospels or some other scriptural texts. Furthermore,
the penance should reflect the seriousness of the sin. For example, in the 11th century, the King of
the Holy Roman Empire, Henry IV, denounced Pope Gregory VII because they had an argument about who
should have let new bishops. So, Pope Gregory excommunicated King Henry. Long story short,
excommunicated kings tend to be overthrown, so, fearing the loss of his kingdom, King Henry made a
trip across the Alps while wearing a hair shirt and walking barefoot in the middle of the winter. He
then waited outside the castle where the Pope was staying for three days. Finally, the Pope absolved
and forgave him. Unfortunately, in the end, King Henry lost his kingdom anyway because he didn't
change his ways. But, hey, that was some serious penance he undertook, right? So what is a good
penance? In short, penance starts when you examine your conscience and realize what sins you have
committed. If you go to confession, you will get a proper penance from a priest, such as prayer,
meditation, a spiritual exercise, or scriptural readings. Afterwards, you can do even more penance,
if you wish, for the love of God. And this will only make your faith stronger. Penance is a great
time for additional penance. Now, you shouldn't go climbing the Alps in a hair shirt without shoes,
but you should do some additional penance this Lent. As our blessed Lord Jesus Christ says so many
times in the book of Revelation, do penance. So what kind of penance are you going to take up this
