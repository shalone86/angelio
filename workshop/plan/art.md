# Art for the videos: who and what each one needs

For the checklist in `videos.yml`. Made Nov 20, 2026, from the draft scripts' plans; it changes as scripts are written.

**Rules that keep this list short**
- **Characters guide; real things get real pictures.** A chalice, an icon, a church, a painting of a saint's life: a
  picture from the free search (his gallery, Wikimedia, museums). Only characters and a few simple props are drawn.
- **New characters are voxel models** (MagicaVoxel, 8 × 8 × 8, one file per pose, uploaded on angelio.org/studio/draw/).
  Each needs only an **idle**; a **walk** is nice for a few. His pixel versions of the same people work until a voxel
  one exists, so nothing here blocks a video.
- **Make a base once, recolor it many times**: a man, a woman, a child, a monk, a saint-in-robes. Most saints and
  apostles are a base with new colors and one telling detail (a beard, a crown, a staff).
- **Candle Rack shares the cast**: the priest, server, families and Eastern priest it needs are the same people the
  videos use. Make them once.
