About Angelio

We're a Catholic family making the learning games we wished we had for our own kids. Each one is tested at our kitchen table first.

Every game works on its own in the browser. Nothing to install (you can add any game to your home screen), no sign-up, and your child's progress stays on your device.

Free for everyone

Every game is free with your device's own voice. If Angelio helps your family or classroom, you can help keep it free for others.
