About Angelio

Angelio is named for the angels, God's messengers and every child's guardian. Our games try to be like that: always nearby, always kind, always ready with a hint.

We're a Catholic family, and every game is tested by our own kids first. Nothing to install (you can add any game to your home screen), no sign-up, and your child's progress stays on your device.
