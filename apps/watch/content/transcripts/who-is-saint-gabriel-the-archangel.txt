Let's learn about St. Gabriel. The Bible has many interesting stories about Gabriel. He appeared in the Bible four times. Gabriel also appeared after Daniel prayed for Israel and told him about a mysterious prophecy. In the Gospels, Gabriel told Zachary about the birth of John the Baptist and he was the angel of the Annunciation who told the Virgin Mary about the birth of Christ. St. Gabriel and St. Michael are two angels that are talked about in many religious stories. Gabriel is known as the angel of God's power and strength while Michael is known for being merciful. In some stories, Gabriel is seen as a comforting angel who helps people feel better. In Jewish stories, Gabriel is known for the opposite things. He's known for punishing bad people and Michael is known for being kind and merciful. Jewish traditions say Gabriel helped destroy Sodom, a very bad city, and he also helped to bury Moses. According to Christian tradition, Gabriel visited St. Joseph and some shepherds during the infancy of Christ. Some people also believe that Gabriel helped Jesus while praying in the Garden of Gethsemane. Even though Gabriel is called an archangel, some say that he and another angel named Raphael are God's most important messengers.
