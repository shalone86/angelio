Do you ever wonder if your prayers make a difference? Are you unsure if they are effective or if they have any impact at all? This is SDKs and Courses. Today's topic is, Does Prayer Make a Difference? Let's begin. Jesus promises whatever you ask in prayer you will receive if you have faith, in Matthew 21. St. James also affirms that the prayer of a righteous man has great power in its effects. That's James chapter 5. Through prayer, we connect with the loving Father who responds to our needs, as Luke chapter 11 verse 13 states, how much more will the Heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to those who ask Him? But some might say, hey, I've prayed for lots of things and I never seem to get them. Well, while we may think we know what we want, James warns us, quote, you ask and do not receive because you ask wrongly to spend it on your passions. And that's from James chapter 4 verse 3. Sometimes we pray for the wrong things and God doesn't want to answer those prayers because He knows what's best for us. So when we don't get what we ask for, it's important to believe that God has a bigger plan for us than we can imagine. When we pray to God, He doesn't change His action towards us, but instead brings about what He had always planned to do, taking our prayers into account. Sometimes God grants our prayers directly or He can do it with a miracle. He can also do it without any help from other sources. Sometimes He grants our prayers indirectly by using natural events, family, friends, or even a stranger to answer our prayers. So what is the effect of prayer? Well, prayer is a helpful practice that can benefit us in many ways. It can help us receive the gifts and graces we need as well as elevate our thoughts and feelings towards God. It can also increase our trust in God and provide us with many other positive emotions. Even if our prayers aren't answered in the way we want, the benefits of prayer are still worth it. Nothing is more valuable than being able to talk with God through prayer. So why not pray more? Talk with God, seek His guidance, find peace, and try to find God's loving plan for your life.
