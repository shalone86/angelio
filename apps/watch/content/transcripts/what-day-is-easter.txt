Welcome to SD Case & Courses where we will explore the date of Easter. For a long time, people argued about when to celebrate Easter. Some thought it should be on a weekday, while others believed it should be on a Sunday. Eventually it was decided that Easter would always be on a Sunday. However even after that, people still had disagreements about how to calculate the date. The most important thing to remember is that the church wanted everyone to celebrate Easter together. Some of the people and councils involved were Pope Sixtus and Saint Polycarp around the year 120, Pope Victor around the year 190, the Council of Nicaea in the year 325, and the Synod of Whitby in the year 664. They worked hard over hundreds of years, many church councils and many debates to find a solution that would work for everyone. Due to their efforts today we celebrate Easter on the first Sunday after the first full moon following the 21st of March. The story of the Easter controversy teaches us an important lesson about working together. Even when people have different opinions, they can still find a way to come together and make something that works for everyone, and that was the date of Easter in a nutshell.
