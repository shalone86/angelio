# Procession!

Clear the road ahead of the parish procession — tap hazards to fix them, or steer **Left / Mid / Right** (`◀ ▶` or `Q / E`) to weave past trouble.

- **5 routes:** Parish Town, Market Day, River Crossing (Washed-Out Bridge), Candlelight Vigil (night + wind gusts), and endless Feast Procession (all 4 bosses + stretch shifts).
- **5 unlockable advance crew:** Sacristan (+1 candle), Hospitality (+25% sprint speed), Groundskeeper (+50% faster fixes), Choir Director (slower road), and Monsignor (Church Bell auto-clears hazards).
- **Controls:** Tap hazards/road or drag/WASD to run; `◀ ▶` / tap procession lane / `Q E` to steer.
- **Rewards:** Voxel stickers (`2★` mini, `3★` giant) + Parish Bells (`🔔`) + `+25 🔔` Today's Challenge bonus.
